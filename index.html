Project 1 · Madre Natura

Madre Natura

Handcrafted in Sydney from natural fibres, in small runs, with lifetime mending.

Industry & location
Small-run womenswear · Sydney

Turn newsletter sign-ups and hesitant shoppers into buyers while protecting full price. The only discount is the 10% welcome offer. Everything else sells through story, materials and mending.

  • 3 Launch campaign
