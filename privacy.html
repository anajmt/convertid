Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ConvertID ("we", "us") is an email marketing agency for fashion and ecommerce brands. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and your choices.

Information you give us

If you send us a message through the contact form, we collect:

  • your name and business email address
  • your brand name and website (optional)
  • your monthly email marketing revenue range (optional)
  • what you need help with, and your message

We use this information only to reply to your enquiry and to arrange a discovery call, a free email scorecard or an email audit.

How the contact form works

Contact form submissions are sent to Formspree, a third-party form service, which forwards them to our inbox. Formspree processes and may store these submissions under its own privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Booking calls

Calls are booked through Calendly. The details you enter when booking are handled by Calendly under its own privacy policy: calendly.com/privacy.

Website hosting and fonts

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies on this website.

Marketing email

We don't add anyone to a marketing list from this website. If you want to hear from us beyond your enquiry, we'll ask you directly.

How long we keep your details

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to reply to you and, if we work together, to manage that project. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live (Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom or Canada), you may have additional rights under local privacy law. To use any of these rights, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sales@convertid.agency.
