Privacy Policy

Draft. This page describes how the contact form works today. It must be reviewed and finalised before the site goes live, including the details of any form service, hosting provider and the laws that apply to your markets (Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada).

What we collect

When you use the contact form or book a call, we collect only the details you choose to send us:

  • Your name and business email address
  • Your brand name and website (optional)
  • Your monthly email marketing revenue range (optional)
  • What you need help with, and your message

How we use it

We use these details to reply to your enquiry and to arrange a discovery call or email audit. We do not use them to send marketing email unless you ask us to.

Booking calls

Calls are booked through Calendly. Calendly handles the details you enter when booking, under its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your details

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and to manage any project that follows. To ask us to delete your details, email support.convertid@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support.convertid@gmail.com.
